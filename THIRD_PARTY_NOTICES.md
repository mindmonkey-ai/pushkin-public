# Third-party notices and verification status

The [project license](LICENSE) applies to Pushkin's own software and documentation.
Dependencies retain their respective licenses and obligations.

This document is not a complete dependency license inventory. No binaries are
included in this documentation bundle. A complete notice inventory and software
bill of materials must be assembled for the exact artifacts before the next
binary publication.

The next binary release also requires an artifact privacy audit and associated
checksums and signatures. Those checks are not completed or certified by this
documentation-only bundle. A listing of archive filenames alone is not a privacy
audit, and the absence of a source archive does not prove that a binary contains
no sensitive strings or build paths.
